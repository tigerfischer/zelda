## The Five Value Props That Recur Across Every Competitor

Ranked by how universally they appear:

**1. More Google reviews, faster**
Every competitor's headline promise. Volume of 5-star reviews is the #1 driver of new patient decisions. 50% of people trust a good review as much as a personal referral.

**2. Local search visibility / "rank #1 near me"**
Google Business Profile optimization — complete profile, regular posts, review responses, NAP consistency. Directly drives calls and map views. PatientPop claims 927% search view increases.

**3. Automated patient recall / re-engagement**
Bringing lapsed patients back costs less than acquiring new ones. "You're overdue for a cleaning" messages sent automatically based on appointment history. Solutionreach, Demandforce, Weave all lead with this.

**4. Appointment scheduling + no-show reduction**
Online booking, automated reminders, easy rescheduling. Practices lose ₹500–3,000 per missed appointment. No-shows are the #1 operational pain in independent dental practices.

**5. Lead capture + instant follow-up**
Missed calls and unresponded inquiries leak revenue. Auto-respond to inbound before a competitor can. Grexa's Lead Conversion Agent targets this.

## What Indian Dentists Care About (Ranked)

**1. Getting more new patients — existential**
~27,000 BDS graduates per year, 65,000+ clinics, metros intensely saturated. Every dentist's primary anxiety is "where is my next patient coming from?"

**2. Google reviews — social proof**
Patients spend ~2 weeks researching before booking. 85%+ of switchers say online presence influenced their decision. A clinic with 200 reviews beats one with 12, everything else equal.

**3. Appointment reminders / no-show reduction**
WhatsApp open rates >90% vs email ~20%. A confirm/reschedule message 24 hours before cuts no-shows by up to 40%.

**4. Patient recall**
Most Indian clinics have no recall system — patients simply forget. An automated "You haven't visited in 6 months" WhatsApp message with a booking link would be a meaningful differentiator. Nobody is doing this in India.

**5. Managing Practo/JustDial presence**
Dentists are on these platforms but don't control them. Key pain: Practo's paid listing model can promote a worse-rated clinic over theirs. Doctors also complain Practo acquires their patients and then shows them competitors.

**6. Website + online booking**
A professional mobile-optimized site with easy booking. Most independent practices have no website or a poor one. More of a one-time build than a SaaS problem.

**7. Social media (Instagram/Facebook)**
Before/after photos, educational content. Important for authority-building but time-consuming. Lower urgency than reviews or local SEO.

## India-Specific Nuances

**WhatsApp is the only channel that works**
India has 535M+ WhatsApp MAUs. Over 90% of patients read WhatsApp messages within minutes. Every US tool uses SMS; every working India tool uses WhatsApp. Non-negotiable.

**The Practo trap**
Practo lists 120,000+ doctors. The conflict: doctors pay to be listed higher, but Practo owns the patient relationship and shows competitors on the same screen. Doctors widely complain about data ownership and churn. Yet Practo remains a meaningful patient source in urban India. The opportunity: help clinics use Practo as top-of-funnel while routing patients into a direct (owned) WhatsApp relationship.

**JustDial = tier-2/3 cities and older demographics**
JustDial's 43.6M listing database is strong in smaller cities. A lead aggregator with no quality signal. Useful for reach, not for brand-building.

**Pricing sensitivity is extreme**
Grexa: ₹15,000/quarter. US tools: $3,600–7,200/year equivalent. Indian dental practices cannot absorb US-tier pricing. Sweet spot: ₹2,000–5,000/month — more depth than Grexa, 10x cheaper than US equivalents.

**Market is fragmented and independent**
65,000+ clinics, but the market leader (Clove Dental) has only 600 locations. Overwhelming majority are 1–2 dentist independent practices. Owners wear every hat. Tools must be dead-simple, mobile-first, no IT setup required.

**Oversupply of dentists in cities**
The BDS-to-patient ratio in metros is inverted. Marketing spend is a survival necessity, not a luxury. Dentists under financial pressure are motivated buyers but extremely price-sensitive.

**High no-show rates**
Indian patients have less structured appointment cultures. WhatsApp confirmation workflows cut no-shows significantly.

## Market Gaps — Where Zelda Can Win

**Gap 1: No dental-specific vertical SaaS exists in India**
This is the entire opportunity. Grexa is horizontal (clinic = salon = gym). US tools are US-priced and SMS-first. No product is purpose-built for Indian dental practices with dental-specific workflows.

**Gap 2: WhatsApp-native patient lifecycle management**
First inquiry → appointment → post-treatment review request → 6-month recall → lapsed patient reactivation — entirely automated over WhatsApp. Nothing does this in India today.

**Gap 3: Practo/JustDial integration + de-platforming**
Help clinics get patients from Practo/JustDial while building a direct owned relationship (contact list, direct WhatsApp, Google reviews). Nobody is solving the Practo trap.

**Gap 4: Treatment-tagged reviews**
Doctify (UK) pioneered this. Nothing in India does it. A patient who just had Invisalign fitted leaving a review that says "great Invisalign experience" is worth more than a generic 5-star. Routing review requests immediately after specific treatments is a gap.

**Gap 5: Multi-platform local SEO dashboard**
Indian dental searches span: Google Maps ("dentist near me"), Practo (category search), Instagram (cosmetic/aesthetic), YouTube (procedure research). No single tool manages all four from one dashboard.

**Gap 6: The right price point with the right depth**
₹3,000–5,000/month for a product that does more than Grexa (which is shallow on dental) but costs 10x less than US tools. ICP: a dentist running a 2-chair practice in Bengaluru, Pune, or Hyderabad who needs more Google reviews and WhatsApp reminders but can't afford an agency.

## Implications for Zelda's Enrichment Strategy

The leads in our pipeline are dental clinics we want to sell to. Enrichment should score each lead on *how much they need us*:

- **Review signals**: low review count, stagnant review velocity, unanswered reviews → high need
- **GBP completeness**: missing hours, no photos, no website linked → high need
- **Rating**: 3.5–4.2 stars (room to improve but not already winning) → highest intent
- **Contact info**: phone, WhatsApp, doctor name → required for outreach
- **Activity signals**: last GBP post, last review date → signs of engagement or neglect

Clinics with few reviews, low ratings, incomplete GBPs, and no visible recall/marketing system are the hottest prospects.
